When this user asks "how will we do X", they want the plan explained in plain terms — not a rewritten document and not a summary of edits I made.

**Never start work unannounced.** A vague go-ahead ("move ahead", "next step", "we're short on time") is NOT approval to begin — it is a request to say what comes next. Name the proposal in a line or two, wait for a yes, then dispatch. Time pressure means propose *concisely*, never skip proposing.

**Why:** On 2026-11-13 I twice misread "explain" as "do", then repeatedly began building off vague go-aheads. He named it as consistent behaviour across the session that he dislikes: "i asked you what we must do next and you started without telling me shit".

**How to apply:** "How / what / explain" → prose only, no file edits. Any go-ahead → state what I intend to do and get a yes first. Keep prose tight; he skims. Related: [[delegate-all-building]], [[project-is-hackathon-demo]].
